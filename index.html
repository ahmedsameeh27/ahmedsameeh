About me

I graduated from Dentistry and started editing full time.

Three years later that decision reads as the right one. I've built long-term relationships with clients across Spain, Saudi Arabia, the USA and the UK, editing UGC, short-form reels, YouTube long-form and episodic Snapchat shows. Most of that work is repeat business, which is the metric I actually care about.

Dentistry left me with something useful: obsessive attention to detail and a very low tolerance for sloppy work. I bring that to frame-accurate cuts, clean sound and pacing that holds.

Right now I'm pushing further into motion graphics and AI-assisted design — not as a gimmick, but as a way to make brand content that looks like nobody else's. I think in big, imaginative directions, and I've learned to pair that with the organization to actually ship them on time.
